Crew safety knowledge base

How environmental hazards can affect crew health

Explanations below describe general physiological mechanisms. They are not diagnoses or vehicle-specific alarm thresholds.

CO₂Elevated carbon dioxide can cause headache, breathlessness, impaired concentration and, at sufficiently high exposure, serious toxicity.MONITOR
O₂Low oxygen availability can cause hypoxia, reduced judgement, confusion and loss of consciousness. Fire risk also depends on oxygen concentration and materials.MONITOR
PRESSUREPressure loss can reduce oxygen partial pressure; rapid decompression can cause barotrauma and other acute hazards.MONITOR
TEMPERATUREExcess heat increases heat strain and dehydration risk; cold exposure can impair dexterity and, if severe, cause hypothermia.MONITOR
HUMIDITYHigh humidity reduces evaporative cooling and can promote condensation; very low humidity can irritate eyes and airways.MONITOR
